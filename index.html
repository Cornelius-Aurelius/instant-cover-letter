03 / GOOD TO KNOW

Built for a better application.

Is this an AI writing service?

It uses a transparent writing template and your own evidence. No external generative AI service or secret API key is required.

Does it make up experience?

No. It only includes skills, achievements and reasons you provide. Review every sentence before applying.

Does it store my personal data?

Nothing is saved by default. You can choose to save or delete a draft locally on your device.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The editor and preview adapt to mobile, tablet, laptop and desktop screens, with touch and keyboard support.
